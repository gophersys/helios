Operator, Rebuilt

A working replica of Ableton Live 12’s Operator — four FM operators, envelopes, LFO, filter and pitch — rendered in plain HTML and played by a Web Audio engine. Every control binds to one address in one parameter tree; the panel is the front end, the tree is the instrument.
