# alignment — the seeded defect

Two dials on the same line sit 5px apart vertically (tops 20 and 25), so their
centres are not level — a 1px tolerance is all `ui.audit.check_level`
allows within a line.

One gap means the gap law has no pair to compare, the 22px gap clears the
crowding floor, and the dials do not touch: the level check is alone here.
